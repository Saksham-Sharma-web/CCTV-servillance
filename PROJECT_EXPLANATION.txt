1. THE BIG PICTURE (HOW IT WORKS IN 5 SECONDS)
================================================================================

1. Camera sends a photo (frame) to your computer 30 times every second.
2. The AI looks at the photo and asks:
   - "Is there a human or car?" -> (Detection)
   - "Which person is this from the previous photo?" -> (Tracking)
   - "Did they cross the yellow line?" -> (Fence Intrusion)
   - "Have they been standing still for too long?" -> (Loitering)
3. If a rule is broken, it rings an alarm (Event).
4. Finally, it draws colored boxes and alerts on your screen so you can see it live.


================================================================================
2. PART 1: FINDING & CONNECTING TO THE CAMERA
================================================================================

[File: discovery.py] -> "The Megaphone"
- When you start the project, your computer doesn't know where the camera is.
- discovery.py uses a special language called "ONVIF WS-Discovery".
- It shouts over your Wi-Fi: "Hey, are there any CCTV or phone cameras here?"
- Your phone/camera replies: "Yes! I am at IP address 192.168.0.108!"

[File: stream.py] -> "The Handshake"
- Once we know the IP address, stream.py connects to it using the username 
  and password (default: cam / 12345678).
- It asks the camera: "Please give me the live video link (RTSP link)."
- The camera gives back the link: rtsp://cam:12345678@192.168.0.108:8554/live.


================================================================================
3. PART 2: THE CONDUCTOR
================================================================================

[File: main.py] -> "The Boss / Orchestrator"
- main.py is the master file you run in your terminal (`python main.py`).
- It does three main jobs in a continuous loop:
  1. Grabs a fresh picture from the camera video feed.
  2. Sends the picture to the AI Brain (IBVAPPipeline) to analyze it.
  3. Opens a video window on your desktop screen showing the camera feed 
     with all the AI boxes, lines, and threat alerts drawn on top!


================================================================================
4. PART 3: THE AI BRAIN (THE STEP-BY-STEP PIPELINE)
================================================================================

Inside the folder `ibvap/`, the pipeline analyzes the image through 8 steps:

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: THE EYES (Object Detection)
[File: ibvap/detection/object_detector.py]
- Uses an AI model named YOLOv8 (You Only Look Once).
- The AI looks at the entire image in one quick glance and finds objects.
- It returns bounding boxes: "Person found at box [x1, y1, x2, y2] with 92% confidence."

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2: THE MEMORY (Persistent Object Tracker)
[File: ibvap/tracking/tracker.py]
- A camera takes 30 photos every second. In each photo, YOLO just sees "a person".
- The Tracker gives the person a name/ID: "This is Person #1".
- As you walk across the room, the tracker remembers: "Person #1 moved 5 steps left."
- It draws a colored tail (trail) behind you showing where you walked.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3: FACE CHECKING (Biometric Verification)
[File: ibvap/face/detector.py & matcher_adapter.py]
- Whenever a person is close enough, the AI crops their face.
- It converts the face into a special mathematical fingerprint (512 numbers).
- It compares it against an authorized list:
  - If match found: "Welcome John Doe (Authorized Employee)."
  - If no match: "UNKNOWN_PERSON."

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4: CAR NUMBER PLATE READING (ANPR)
[File: ibvap/anpr/plate_detector.py & ocr_adapter.py]
- If a car or motorcycle is detected, the AI crops the license plate.
- It uses Optical Character Recognition (OCR) to read the letters and numbers.
- Checks against security watchlists:
  - Whitelist: Allowed vehicle.
  - Blacklist: DANGER! Stolen or banned vehicle alert!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 5: INVISIBLE TRIPWIRES & PERIMETERS (Virtual Fence)
[File: ibvap/analytics/virtual_fence.py]
- You can draw invisible lines or zones on the camera feed.
- Center Yellow Line: Acts as a tripwire.
- Orange Zone: Acts as a restricted high-security area.
- If the AI sees Person #1's foot step across the line, it immediately flags:
  🚨 "FENCE_INTRUSION: Person crossed the border line!"

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 6: THE DETECTIVE (Behavioral Analytics & Loitering)
[File: ibvap/analytics/suspicious_activity.py]
- The AI watches what people are DOING, not just where they are.
- Loitering: If Person #1 stands in one spot for more than 5 seconds without moving,
  it flags: ⚠️ "LOITERING DETECTED!"
- Suspicious Movement: If someone suddenly sprints or moves erratically at night,
  it flags: ⚠️ "SUSPICIOUS_MOVEMENT DETECTED!"

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 7: THE ALARM BELL (Event Deduplication Engine)
[File: ibvap/events/event_engine.py]
- If someone stands in front of the camera for 10 seconds, we don't want 300 alarms 
  spammed every second!
- The Event Engine "debounces" alerts: It triggers the alarm once, logs it, 
  and waits for a cooldown before repeating.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 8: THE PAINTER (Visual Debug Renderer)
[File: ibvap/visualization/debug_renderer.py]
- Takes the raw camera picture and paints all the AI graphics on it:
  - 🟩 Neon Green Boxes around detected humans (e.g. `PERSON #1`)
  - 🟨 Yellow Tripwire Line across the screen
  - 🟧 Semi-transparent Orange Perimeter Box
  - 🚨 Big Red Flashing Alert Banner across the top when a threat happens
  - 📊 Live FPS (frames per second) counter at the bottom

5. FILE-BY-FILE SUMMARY CHEAT SHEET
================================================================================

- main.py                          -> The master program you run.
- discovery.py                     -> Finds cameras automatically on Wi-Fi.
- connection.py & stream.py        -> Logs in & gets RTSP video link from camera.
- yolov8n.pt                       -> The pre-trained AI neural network brain.
- requirements.txt                 -> List of required Python packages.

Inside folder /ibvap:
- ibvap/core/pipeline.py           -> Master orchestration that connects all AI steps.
- ibvap/core/types.py              -> Data contracts (Detection, Track, Event).
- ibvap/core/config.py             -> Settings (confidence thresholds, timers).
- ibvap/detection/                 -> YOLOv8 object detector.
- ibvap/tracking/                  -> Remembers people IDs across frames.
- ibvap/face/                      -> Face detection and identity matching.
- ibvap/anpr/                      -> Car number plate reader.
- ibvap/analytics/virtual_fence.py -> Virtual tripwires and restricted zones.
- ibvap/analytics/suspicious_activity.py -> Loitering and suspicious behavior rules.
- ibvap/events/event_engine.py     -> Clean alarm management and deduplication.
- ibvap/visualization/             -> Draws boxes, lines, and HUD on video.
